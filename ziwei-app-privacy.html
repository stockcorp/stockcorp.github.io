「紫微斗數排盤」Android App 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 21 日

1. 適用範圍與提供者

本政策適用於 Android App「紫微斗數排盤 - AI 命盤解析」（套件名稱 tool.ziwei，以下稱本 App），由自由數位科技有限公司（Liberty Digital Technology Co., Ltd.，以下稱本公司）提供。使用本 App 也適用本公司的使用者條款。

2. 我們處理的資料

  • 命盤檔案（只存在手機）：你輸入的稱呼、性別、出生日期與時間，以及排出的命盤與解盤紀錄，都只儲存在你的手機，不會上傳保存。清除 App 資料或解除安裝即刪除。排盤完全在手機本機計算。
  • AI 解盤（你主動使用時才傳送）：當你按下「開始解盤」，本 App 會把這張命盤的資料（性別、農曆出生年月日與時辰、五行局、十二宮星曜配置、選擇的解盤項目與流年年份）以及一組隨機產生的安裝識別碼，透過 HTTPS 傳送到本公司伺服器（cc.ldigi.com.tw），再由本公司伺服器交給 OpenAI 的 AI 服務產生解說文字後回傳。不會傳送你的稱呼。本公司伺服器不儲存你的出生資料與解盤內容，只為了防止濫用而短暫保存使用次數（以安裝識別碼與經雜湊處理的 IP 代碼計數，約 3 天後刪除）。
  • 購買資料：點數包與「移除廣告」透過 Google Play 付款，付款資訊由 Google 處理，本公司不會取得你的信用卡資料。點數餘額存在手機本機。
  • 廣告資料（Google AdMob）：本 App 顯示 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告，以及你主動點選才播放的獎勵式影片廣告。Google 可能收集廣告 ID、裝置與 App 資訊、IP 位址（可推估大略位置）及廣告互動紀錄，用於投放與衡量廣告、防止詐欺。

3. 裝置權限

  • 網際網路（INTERNET）、網路狀態（ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE）：AI 解盤與顯示廣告。
  • Google Play 帳款（BILLING）：購買點數包與移除廣告。
  • 廣告 ID（AD_ID）：供 AdMob 投放與衡量廣告。你可以在手機「設定 → Google → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID。

本 App 不使用相機、麥克風、定位、聯絡人或檔案存取權限。

4. 蒐集目的與利用

依個人資料保護法第 5、19、20 條，上述資料只用於提供排盤與 AI 解盤、處理購買、顯示廣告與防止濫用。本公司不出售你的個人資料。

5. 第三方服務

  • OpenAI（產生解盤文字）：OpenAI 隱私權政策
  • Google AdMob、使用者訊息平台（UMP）與 Google Play 帳款服務：Google 隱私權政策、Google 如何使用廣告技術

這些服務可能在台灣以外的地區處理資料。

6. 資料保存與刪除（紫微斗數排盤／自由數位科技有限公司）

命盤檔案與解盤紀錄只在你的手機：可在 App 內逐筆刪除，或透過「設定 → 應用程式 → 紫微斗數排盤 → 儲存空間 → 清除資料」全部刪除。本公司伺服器不保存你的出生資料與解盤內容；防濫用的使用次數紀錄約 3 天後自動刪除。如需提前刪除或有其他資料請求，請寄信至 service@ldigi.com.tw，標題寫「紫微斗數排盤 刪除資料」，我們會在 30 天內處理並回覆。

7. 您的權利

你可以依個人資料保護法第 3 條，請求查詢、閱覽、製給複本、補充更正、停止蒐集處理利用或刪除你的個人資料。歐盟／英國使用者首次開啟時會看到廣告同意聲明，之後可從 App「設定 → 廣告隱私權選項」變更；美國加州使用者享有 CCPA 規定的權利。

8. 資料安全

App 與伺服器之間以 HTTPS 加密連線，請求帶有簽章驗證。任何網路傳輸都無法保證絕對安全，我們會以合理方式保護資料。

9. 特別聲明

本 App 的排盤與解盤內容屬於傳統命理文化之娛樂參考，不構成醫療、心理、投資、法律或任何專業建議，請勿據以做出重大決定。

10. 兒童隱私

本 App 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。未成年人使用前應取得法定代理人同意。

Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart Android app – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Scope and provider

This policy covers the Android app "Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart – AI Reading" (package tool.ziwei, "the App"), provided by Liberty Digital Technology Co., Ltd. ("we"). Our Terms of Use also apply.

2. Data we handle

  • Chart profiles (on your phone only): the nickname, gender, birth date and time you enter, the charts and your reading history are stored only on your phone and are not uploaded for storage. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling deletes them. Charts are calculated entirely on the device.
  • AI reading (sent only when you use it): when you tap "Start reading", the App sends that chart's data (gender, lunar birth date and hour, element class, the stars in the twelve palaces, the topics you chose and the year) together with a randomly generated installation ID over HTTPS to our server (cc.ldigi.com.tw), which passes it to OpenAI's AI service to generate the text and returns it. Your nickname is not sent. Our server does not store your birth data or the reading; it only keeps short-lived usage counts (by installation ID and a hashed IP code, deleted after about 3 days) to prevent abuse.
  • Purchases: point packs and "Remove ads" are paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment details; we never receive your card data. Your point balance is stored on the phone.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): the App shows a Google AdMob banner and rewarded video ads that play only when you choose to watch them. Google may collect the advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (which can indicate approximate location) and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

3. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: AI readings and ads.
  • Google Play Billing: buying point packs and removing ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob to serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete it in Settings → Google → Ads.

The App does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access.

4. Purpose of use

We use this data only to provide charts and AI readings, process purchases, show ads and prevent abuse. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Third-party services

  • OpenAI (generates the reading text): OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob, User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Google Play Billing: Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses advertising technology

These services may process data outside Taiwan.

6. Retention and deletion (Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart / Liberty Digital Technology Co., Ltd.)

Chart profiles and reading history exist only on your phone: delete them one by one in the App, or all at once in Settings → Apps → Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart → Storage → Clear data. Our server does not keep your birth data or readings; anti-abuse usage counts are deleted automatically after about 3 days. For earlier deletion or any other request, email service@ldigi.com.tw with the subject "Zi Wei chart data deletion" and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, copy, correct, stop the processing of, or delete your personal data. Users in the EU/UK see an ad consent form on first launch and can change it later in the App (Settings → Ad privacy options). California residents have the rights described in the CCPA.

8. Security

The App and our server communicate over HTTPS with signed requests. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect data.

9. Special notice

Charts and readings are traditional-culture entertainment only. They are not medical, psychological, investment, legal or other professional advice and should not be the basis for important decisions.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Minors should have a parent or guardian's consent.

Updates will be posted on this page with a new date above.

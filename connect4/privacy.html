隱私權政策與廣告說明

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 18 日

1. 關於本網站

「四子棋」是一款免費的網頁遊戲，網站託管於 GitHub Pages。

2. 我們收集哪些資料

本遊戲不需要註冊，不收集姓名、電子郵件等個人資料。遊戲進度、設定、語言偏好與戰績只儲存在你瀏覽器的 localStorage，不會上傳；清除瀏覽器資料即可刪除。

2-1. 全球排行榜（自願）

遊戲結束時，若你選擇「上傳到全球排行榜」，我們會把你輸入的暱稱、成績、遊戲名稱與難度，以及經過雜湊處理、無法還原的 IP 代碼，儲存在本公司伺服器（game.ldigi.com.tw），用來顯示排名與防止濫用。暱稱會公開顯示，請勿輸入真實姓名或個人資料。開局時瀏覽器會向該伺服器取得一次性的開局憑證。如需刪除你的排行榜紀錄，請透過 Google Play 開發人員頁面上的聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

3. Google AdSense 廣告與 Cookie（網頁版）

網頁版使用 Google AdSense 顯示廣告。Google 及其合作夥伴可能使用 Cookie 或類似技術，依你造訪本網站或其他網站的紀錄投放廣告。你可以到 Google 廣告設定管理或關閉個人化廣告，詳情請參考 Google 如何使用廣告技術。

4. 線上對戰

使用線上對戰時，你的瀏覽器會連到 PeerJS 公用配對伺服器（0.peerjs.com）與 Google、Cloudflare 的 STUN 伺服器來建立點對點（WebRTC）連線。連線建立後，對局資料直接在你和對手的裝置之間傳送，雙方的 IP 位址會被對方的瀏覽器得知。我們不保存房號、對局或聊天表情紀錄。

5. App 版本

若本遊戲以 App 形式提供，App 內不載入 AdSense；App 若有廣告或其他第三方服務，以該 App 在商店頁面公布的隱私權政策為準。

6. 第三方服務

GitHub Pages 可能依其隱私權政策記錄基本連線資訊（例如 IP 位址）。除上述服務外，本網站未使用其他分析或追蹤工具。

7. 兒童

本遊戲並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

8. 更新

本政策如有更新會公布在本頁。

Privacy Policy and Advertising

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. About this site

"Connect Four" is a free browser game hosted on GitHub Pages.

2. What we collect

No account is needed and we do not collect names, email addresses or other personal information. Your progress, settings, language and stats are stored only in your browser's localStorage and are never uploaded. Clear your browser data to delete them.

2-1. Global leaderboard (optional)

When a game ends and you choose to submit to the global leaderboard, we store the nickname you enter, your score, the game and level, and a hashed, non-reversible code derived from your IP address on our server (game.ldigi.com.tw) to show rankings and prevent abuse. Nicknames are public, so do not use your real name or personal information. When a game starts, your browser requests a one-time game token from that server. To delete your leaderboard entries, contact us through the details on our Google Play developer page.

3. Google AdSense and cookies (web version)

The web version shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings. See how Google uses advertising technology.

4. Online play

When you play online, your browser connects to the public PeerJS matchmaking server (0.peerjs.com) and to Google and Cloudflare STUN servers to set up a peer-to-peer (WebRTC) connection. Once connected, game data travels directly between you and your opponent, and each browser can see the other's IP address. We do not store room codes, games or emotes.

5. App versions

If this game is offered as an app, AdSense is not loaded inside the app. Any ads or third-party services in the app are covered by the privacy policy published on its store page.

6. Third-party services

GitHub Pages may log basic connection data (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy. Apart from the services above, this site uses no other analytics or tracking tools.

7. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
